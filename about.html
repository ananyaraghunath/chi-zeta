Our story.

Who we are

A second family made up of the diverse students at NJIT.

Chi Zeta brings together inspiring individuals from every major on campus and gives them somewhere to become sharper, more confident and better connected than they could alone.

Through professional programming, community service and a rush process that genuinely asks something of you, our members build the skills formal education leaves out: compromise, consensus, project management, and a network that answers when you call.

Our timeline

1904

Alpha Kappa Psi is founded at New York University — the first professional business fraternity in the United States.

2017

The Chi Zeta chapter is chartered at the New Jersey Institute of Technology, and a Founding Class of 48 is initiated.

Today

197 brothers and alumni across 19 classes, from the Founding Class through Sigma.

What we build

Professional development

Don’t just stand up. Stand out.

Programming that keeps skills sharp — the Principled Business Leadership Institute, The Academy, Case Competition, and the College of Leadership.

Experience

Reach where you desire to go.

Members use what the classroom teaches to acquire what it does not: compromise, consensus building, project management, team building and networking.

Brotherhood

Be in the company of ambition.

Meaningful, lifelong friendships — from fellow students to local business leaders and faculty — uniting members regardless of age, location and experience.

Teamwork

It’s what makes the dream work.

An understanding of service, an appreciation of diversity, and the critical thinking to overcome challenges — a skill that ranks very high among recruiters.
