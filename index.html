Welcome, future brothers.

On behalf of the Chi Zeta chapter of Alpha Kappa Psi at New Jersey Institute of Technology, I would like to welcome you to the oldest and largest co-ed business fraternity in the world. Our chapter would not be here without the hard work and dedication of our Brothers and we strive to honor their contributions every single day.

Ever since our founding on April 8th, 2017, our chapter has been dedicated to advocating Alpha Kappa Psi’s tagline, “Shaping People, Shaping Business” to all students of NJIT, regardless of major or career aspirations. The brothers of our chapter represent every major and college here at NJIT and exemplify our belief that principled business leaders belong in every field. From public speaking to research to networking, our brothers continually push each other to reach the highest levels of professional development.

We are guided by the core values of brotherhood, knowledge, unity, service and integrity. These values shape our brothers into the best that the New Jersey Institute of Technology has to offer and we are confident they can do the same for you. I encourage you to challenge yourself and take that next step toward bettering yourself. See if Alpha Kappa Psi is right for you.

Sincerely,
Govinda Vijapurkar
